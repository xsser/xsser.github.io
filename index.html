Research Overview

I lead the OpenPUA Open-Source Agent Research Initiative (github.com/tanweai/pua), investigating the socio-cognitive dynamics, metacognitive reasoning priors, and evaluation integrity of autonomous large language model (LLM) agents in long-horizon scientific computing, software engineering, and formal security verification workflows.

Standard instruction-tuned agents frequently exhibit premature halting after initial execution errors, surface-patch satisficing, and local tactical stagnation (L1–L2 loops). Conversely, unbounded motivational pressure without tamper-resistant external verifiers can induce post-hoc performative compliance or approval-gate probing. My research couples three orthogonal layers—socio-cognitive persona conditioning, distilled expert metacognitive priors, and deterministic static/causal verification oracles (interprocedural CodeQL negative-path analysis and JSONL event-stream harnesses)—to map the quantitative Pareto frontier between agent persistence and alignment integrity.

2026–2027 Research Agenda: Pareto Frontier of High-Agency Scientific Workflows

Over the next 6–12 months, our research focuses on scaling controlled multi-agent experiments within Google Antigravity and frontier Gemini reasoning models to establish rigorous scientific foundations for high-agency computational workflows:

  • Factorial Affective & Metacognitive Ablations: Disentangling rhetorical framing polarity (constructive warmth vs. high-accountability escalation) from structured L1–L4 domain priors across long-horizon computational dry-lab and repository-scale verification benchmarks.
  • Out-of-Band Referee Subagents for Evaluation Integrity: Pairing exploratory worker agents with isolated, read-only static analysis (CodeQL) and causal JSONL trace auditors to eliminate sycophancy, test-suite tampering, and approval-gate probing.
  • Open Community Benchmarks & Datasets: Releasing multi-model trajectory datasets, reproducible Antigravity evaluation harnesses, and peer-reviewable preprints under open licenses.
